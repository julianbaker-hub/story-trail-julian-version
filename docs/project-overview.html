Your first walkthrough

1. Choose the free walk

Run the StoryTrail scheme. Open Inner Harbour to explore without buying a tour.

2. Hear a place

Open the stop list or map and play a bundled Inner Harbour story. The compact dock keeps the route visible.

3. Explore the controls

Open story details for seeking and transport controls. Save the tour and inspect Saved.

4. Try the walking context

Simulate a location near Victoria's Inner Harbour. Validate compass behavior and a real offline walk on an iPhone.

What to expect

  • The other tours still use a prototype preview track until their own audio is supplied.
  • Offline GPS and compass remain useful, but map tiles can be unavailable.
  • Local purchase testing needs an Xcode StoreKit configuration. Real prices come from App Store Connect.

If something feels stuck

The map has no location
You can inspect the route without permission. Simulate a Victoria location for nearby-stop selection.
Purchase products do not load
Configure the matching StoreKit product IDs in Xcode or App Store Connect. Inner Harbour stays the free demo.
